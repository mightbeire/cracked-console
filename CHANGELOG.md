Initial Community Edition.

- Today execution;
- curriculum browsing and search;

- evidence records;
- project milestones;

- no required account;
- no required hosted backend;
- no required API key;
- no mandatory AI service;
- no XP or streaks;
- user-owned configuration and execution records.
